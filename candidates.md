## Selection

**Winner: #23 — 24/25, 110 chars.** The top two tie at 24/25, and the tie
breaks by the brief's own decision rule: go through the criteria in order and
let the first one where they differ decide. #21 and #23 tie on C1 (5–5) and C2
(5–5); they differ first on C3, where #23 is the only 5 — its final word,
"unionized", IS the punchline, while #21's punch ("jam, but louder") sits
mid-line and its final words are a timestamp. #23 also uses one of the brief's
own named details (the coffee machine) and escalates over three beats —
Monday break, Tuesday support group, Wednesday unionized — so the laugh
arrives entirely on the final beat.

**Why #36 (22/25) loses despite the shortest text:** it turns on a corporate
cliché phrase ("wearing many hats") rather than a concrete office detail
(C2 4), and the hats-vs-salary contrast circulates as a meme (C5 3).
